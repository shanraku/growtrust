03 / FAIR DISPUTE PROCESS

When results or delivery fall short

1Review the agreement

Check the package scope, baseline, exclusions, measurement source and promised dates.

2Collect comparable evidence

Save dated public metric observations and delivery records. Allow for platform audits, removals and normal fluctuations.

3Hear both sides

A real support team should let the customer explain the issue and the seller respond before deciding.

4Apply the written remedy

Use the agreed policy: correction, replacement work, partial refund or another proportionate outcome.

Important: Follower and view counts can change for reasons outside a seller’s control. GrowTrust must not promise permanent metrics. Any retention commitment must define the metric, baseline, source, time window, exclusions and remedy before an order is placed.
